question: "How often should I visit the dentist?"
category: "general"
order: 1
---

We recommend visiting Dentara every **6 months** for a routine cleaning and examination. Patients with gum disease, a history of frequent cavities, or certain medical conditions may benefit from more frequent visits — typically every 3–4 months. Your dentist will recommend the right schedule for your specific situation during your first appointment.

question: "Does teeth whitening damage enamel?"
category: "cosmetic"
order: 2
---

When performed correctly, professional teeth whitening does not damage enamel. The active ingredients (hydrogen or carbamide peroxide) temporarily open enamel pores to remove staining — this is reversible and safe under professional supervision. Over-the-counter products used excessively can cause issues, which is why we recommend professional treatments with proper dosage and timing.

question: "At what age should my child have their first dental visit?"
category: "pediatric"
order: 3
---

The American Academy of Pediatric Dentistry recommends a child's first dental visit **by their first birthday** or within 6 months of their first tooth appearing. Early visits establish a positive relationship with dental care, allow us to monitor development, and give parents guidance on cleaning baby teeth and avoiding bottle-related decay.

question: "Is Invisalign as effective as traditional braces?"
category: "orthodontic"
order: 4
---

For mild to moderate alignment issues, Invisalign is highly effective — comparable to traditional braces. For more severe cases involving significant crowding, large gaps, or complex bite corrections, traditional braces typically deliver more precise results. The best option depends on your specific case, which we assess during a free consultation.

question: "What should I do if I have a dental emergency?"
category: "emergency"
order: 5
---

Call us immediately — we prioritize same-day appointments for dental emergencies. For a knocked-out tooth, keep it moist (in milk or saline), handle it only by the crown (not the root), and see us within 30–60 minutes for the best chance of saving it. For severe pain, take over-the-counter pain relief as directed on the packaging and avoid placing aspirin directly on gum tissue.

question: "How long does teeth whitening last?"
category: "cosmetic"
order: 6
---

In-office professional whitening results typically last **12–24 months** with proper care. Take-home professional kits last 6–12 months. Results vary based on diet, oral hygiene habits, and lifestyle factors (coffee, tea, wine, tobacco significantly accelerate re-staining). We provide a take-home maintenance kit with all in-office treatments to extend your results.

question: "Is dental treatment painful?"
category: "general"
order: 8
---

Modern dentistry is remarkably comfortable. We use topical anesthetics before any injection, and our local anesthesia protocols ensure you feel nothing during procedures. For patients with dental anxiety, we offer nitrous oxide (laughing gas) sedation to help you relax. Our team is trained to communicate at every step so you always feel in control.
