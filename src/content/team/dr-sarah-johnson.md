Dr. Sarah Johnson leads Dentara's clinical team with a philosophy rooted in patient-centered care. Known for her gentle chairside manner and meticulous attention to detail, she has helped thousands of patients achieve healthier, more confident smiles.

A graduate of Harvard School of Dental Medicine, Dr. Johnson completed advanced training in implant dentistry and cosmetic procedures before founding Dentara. She is a member of the American Dental Association and the Academy of Cosmetic Dentistry.

Outside the practice, she volunteers with community dental health programs serving underserved populations.
