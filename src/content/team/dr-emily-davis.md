Dr. Emily Davis is Dentara's cosmetic and restorative specialist, combining artistic vision with technical mastery to create stunning smile transformations. She holds a Fellowship with the American Academy of Cosmetic Dentistry — a distinction earned by fewer than 5% of cosmetic dentists worldwide.

Dr. Davis approaches each case as a collaboration, taking time to understand the patient's aesthetic goals and designing results that feel natural. She is particularly renowned for her porcelain veneer work and complete smile makeovers.

She lectures regularly at dental conferences and mentors dental students in cosmetic techniques.
