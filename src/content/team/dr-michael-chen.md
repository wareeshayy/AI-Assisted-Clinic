Dr. Michael Chen brings a decade of specialized orthodontic expertise to Dentara. He completed a dual master's program in orthodontics at New York University, developing particular skill in treating complex cases with both traditional braces and the latest clear aligner systems.

His approach combines technical precision with a genuine warmth that puts both children and adults at ease. Dr. Chen is certified in multiple clear aligner platforms and regularly trains other dentists in orthodontic techniques.

He is fluent in English and Mandarin, making care accessible to a wider community.
