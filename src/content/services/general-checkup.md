## Prevention is the Best Medicine

A regular dental checkup is the cornerstone of long-term oral health. At Dentara, we recommend visiting every 6 months for a cleaning and exam. Our thorough approach catches small issues before they become expensive, painful problems.

## What's Included

### Comprehensive Examination
Our dentists assess the health of your teeth, gums, jaw, and oral tissues. We check for cavities, gum disease, bite alignment, and signs of oral cancer.

### Professional Cleaning (Prophylaxis)
Our hygienists remove plaque and tartar buildup that regular brushing can't reach, then polish teeth to a smooth, clean finish.

### Digital X-Rays
Low-radiation digital X-rays provide a clear picture of what's happening beneath the surface — between teeth and below the gum line.

### Oral Cancer Screening
A quick, painless visual and tactile examination of the mouth, tongue, and throat tissues.

### Personalized Advice
We'll discuss your home care routine and provide tailored recommendations for diet, technique, and products.

## How Often Should You Visit?

Most patients benefit from biannual visits. Patients with gum disease, high cavity risk, or certain health conditions may need quarterly visits.
