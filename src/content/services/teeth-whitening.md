## Restore Your Smile's Natural Brilliance

Professional teeth whitening at Dentara is one of the most popular and effective ways to enhance your smile. Unlike over-the-counter products, our clinical-grade whitening treatments are designed to produce dramatic results safely.

## What to Expect

Our whitening process uses a high-concentration bleaching gel activated by a specialized light. Most patients see results **up to 8 shades lighter** in a single session lasting approximately 60–90 minutes.

### Benefits
- Immediate, visible results
- Safe and controlled procedure
- Long-lasting brightness (12–24 months with proper care)
- Custom-fitted trays for take-home maintenance
- Minimal sensitivity with our desensitizing protocol

## Who Is It For?

Teeth whitening is ideal for patients with staining caused by coffee, tea, wine, tobacco, or natural aging. We recommend a preliminary cleaning and exam to ensure you're a good candidate.

## Aftercare

To maintain your results, avoid staining foods and beverages for 48 hours post-treatment and practice consistent oral hygiene. We'll provide a take-home whitening kit for touch-ups.
