## Your Perfect Smile, Designed for You

Cosmetic dentistry at Dentara combines artistry with advanced dental science. Our specialists take time to understand your aesthetic goals, facial symmetry, and natural tooth structure to design a smile that's uniquely yours.

## Treatments

### Porcelain Veneers
Ultra-thin porcelain shells bonded to the front of teeth to correct chips, cracks, gaps, and discoloration. Results last 10–15 years with proper care.

### Dental Bonding
Tooth-colored composite resin applied to repair minor imperfections. A quick, cost-effective solution with same-day results.

### Smile Makeover
A comprehensive treatment plan combining multiple procedures — whitening, veneers, crowns, and orthodontics — to achieve a complete transformation.

### Gum Contouring
Reshaping the gum line to improve the balance between teeth and gums, creating a more harmonious smile.

## The Consultation Process

Your smile design journey begins with a digital preview. Using imaging technology, we'll show you what your new smile will look like before any treatment begins.
