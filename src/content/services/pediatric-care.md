## Caring for Smiles at Every Age

Dentara's family dentistry approach means your entire household can receive comprehensive care under one roof. Our pediatric specialists create a warm, welcoming environment that makes children feel comfortable and safe — setting the foundation for a lifetime of good oral health.

## Pediatric Services

### Baby Teeth & First Visits
We recommend the first dental visit by age 1 or when the first tooth appears. Early visits establish positive associations with dental care.

### Cleanings & Exams
Regular professional cleanings every 6 months, plus fluoride treatments and sealants to protect against cavities.

### Orthodontic Screening
Early detection of alignment issues allows for timely intervention, often simplifying future treatment.

## Family Care

We treat all ages — from toddlers to seniors. Coordinating care for the whole family is easier when everyone sees the same trusted team.

## Our Child-Friendly Approach

- Gentle, patient communication
- Child-sized instruments and equipment
- Reward systems and positive reinforcement
- Parents welcome in the treatment room
- No-shame, no-fear philosophy
