## Straight Teeth, Confident Smile

Orthodontic treatment at Dentara goes beyond aesthetics — properly aligned teeth are easier to clean, reducing the risk of decay and gum disease. Our certified orthodontists develop personalized treatment plans for patients of all ages.

## Treatment Options

### Traditional Braces
Time-tested metal brackets and wires, ideal for complex alignment corrections. Modern braces are more comfortable and effective than ever before.

### Clear Aligners
Nearly invisible removable aligners that gradually shift teeth into position. Perfect for adults and teens who prefer a discreet option.

### Ceramic Braces
Tooth-colored brackets that blend with your natural smile, offering the effectiveness of traditional braces with improved aesthetics.

## Treatment Timeline

Most orthodontic treatments range from **12 to 24 months**, depending on the complexity of your case. We'll provide a detailed timeline during your initial consultation.
