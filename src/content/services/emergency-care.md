## Dental Emergencies, Handled Swiftly

Dental emergencies can happen at any time. At Dentara, we prioritize urgent cases to ensure you receive prompt attention and relief. Our emergency services are available during extended hours to minimize your discomfort and address the issue quickly.

## What Qualifies as a Dental Emergency?

- **Severe toothache** — persistent, intense pain
- **Knocked-out tooth** — time is critical (within 30–60 minutes)
- **Cracked or broken tooth** — from trauma or biting hard objects
- **Lost filling or crown** — leaving sensitive tissue exposed
- **Dental abscess** — infection that can spread without treatment
- **Soft tissue injuries** — cuts or lacerations to gums, cheeks, or tongue

## What to Do Before You Arrive

**Knocked-out tooth:** Keep the tooth moist (in milk or saline), handle only by the crown, and call us immediately.

**Severe pain:** Take over-the-counter pain relief as directed and avoid placing aspirin directly on gums.

**Broken tooth:** Rinse your mouth with warm water and apply a cold compress to reduce swelling.

## Our Promise

We'll assess your situation over the phone and guide you through next steps. Same-day appointments are our priority for all emergencies.
